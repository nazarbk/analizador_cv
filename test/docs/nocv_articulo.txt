Variational quantum eigensolvers on noisy hardware: a benchmark study
A. García, B. Smith, C. Liu
Abstract
Variational quantum eigensolvers (VQE) are among the most promising algorithms for near-term quantum devices. In this work we benchmark three ansatz families on superconducting hardware and analyse the effect of readout error mitigation. Our results show that hardware-efficient ansätze converge faster but are more sensitive to noise.
Keywords: quantum computing, VQE, error mitigation, NISQ
